## Findings

### 1. NIT: the `failed` scan set is cleared only when the connection leaves `ready`, so an error raised while offline sticks until the next outage
Where: ARCHITECTURE > Media index scan ("cleared together with `current`"; Live upkeep: "`current` is cleared (together with `failed`) whenever `updateConnectionState` leaves `ready`").
Problem: "While the client is still `ready`" means auth `ready`, the sense used everywhere else in the doc. Take a page that errors during a connection outage, after `updateConnectionState` has already left `ready` and cleared the sets. That chat goes into `failed` and stays there through the reconnect. It is not rescanned until the next outage or a restart. The review-4 response and the PROGRESS decision log both promise "one new try after the next reconnect". Impact is low: TDLib usually queues history requests while offline rather than failing them.
Fix: change the Live upkeep sentence to: "`current` is cleared whenever `updateConnectionState` leaves `ready`; `failed` is cleared whenever it enters `ready`." Clearing `failed` on leaving would also re-allow a restart loop while offline, so clear it on entry only.

### 2. NIT: an upload TDLib is still sending after a restart has no live state, so it holds no slot and shows no progress
Where: ARCHITECTURE > Transfer engine > Pump ("`free` = the kind's limit minus its jobs with live state", new in review 4); Upload step 6 ("`sendingStatePending` → keep waiting").
Problem: after a crash, TDLib resumes a pending upload at startup. The job's routing entries are rebuilt, but nothing gives it live state. Under the new `free` rule it does not count against `maxUploads`, so with the default of 1 the pump starts a second upload next to it. Its `updateFile` events are also unrouted, because only `inFlight` maps file ids to jobs, so the Queue row shows a frozen bar until it settles. Nothing is posted twice and the row still settles, so this is throughput and cosmetics only.
Fix (pick one):
- Add one sentence to Upload step 6: "A pending id in `sendingStatePending` gives its job live state: the file id from that message's content (`extractMedia(m).file.id`) goes into `inFlight`, so `updateFile` drives its progress and it counts against `free`."
- Or keep the behavior and add `// ponytail: an upload resumed by TDLib after a crash shows no progress and holds no slot; upgrade: give it live state from getMessage` in Upload step 6 and the PROGRESS deferred list.

## Verified assumptions

Checked by reading the docs:
- The sender check never compares origins. It compares whole pages with hash and query stripped, as decoded case-folded paths for `file:` and lowercased `href`s for the dev URL. Every rejection path (null frame, disposed frame, `new URL` throw, `fileURLToPath` throw) yields 403, not 500, through the `try/catch` around `pageKey(e.senderFrame.url)`.
- From WHATWG URL and Node `url` semantics (not run): `new URL('http://localhost:5173').href` ends in `/`; assigning `''` to `hash`/`search` removes the `#`/`?`; `fileURLToPath` on Windows decodes percent-escapes, returns `c:\…` for `file:///c:/…`, and throws on an encoded `/` or `\`. So the four listed unit cases hold, and percent-encoding differences between the two canonicalizers cannot cause a mismatch after decoding.
- `rendererKey` comes from the same `rendererUrl` passed to `win.loadURL`, and the env URL is ignored when packaged.
- The upload ready-settle rule reads the same in Upload step 6, the invariant, Client lifecycle, the call map, PRODUCT Uploads, PROGRESS Phase 3, and the `settleUpload` test. It does not conflict with step 5 or the `jobs.action` "no live state" note (a row left `active` after logout is handled by pause/cancel until the next `ready`, then settled).
- The cancel cleanup predicate matches the call map row and PRODUCT ("Canceling a download deletes its partial data").
- Status line says "revised after design reviews 1 to 4"; PROGRESS ticks "Design review 4 (4 findings) resolved"; the decision log and changelog have review-4 entries.
